## Claims: what I could and couldn't verify (web check, 25 Nov)
Verified (and how it is shown)

- 2.8 million UK adults planned to play on day one = pre-launch poll → shown as "PROJECTED".
- John Lewis +62% / Currys PS5 +149% (checked earlier in the session).
- Downing Street: "a British success story made in Edinburgh", "his diary is very full, he won't be playing it", PM = Andy Burnham → Express & Star / PA.
- 212 GB pre-load (Rockstar-confirmed) → download bar.
- Schools: Arbor provisional figures — ~35,000 more secondary pupils absent Thursday vs Mon–Wed; morning registers from 1,400+ secondary schools; voucher prize draws are real (St John's Bishop Auckland, Ninestiles in Birmingham, others).

Needs your call (not found / contradicted — the audio says them, the graphics follow the audio, so these are for you to fix or accept)
